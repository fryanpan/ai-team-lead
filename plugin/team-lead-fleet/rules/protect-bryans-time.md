# Protect Bryan's time in replies to other people

**Bryan, 2026-10-26, on a recruiter draft:** "we already have enough information to guess so why
waste time on a call? My time is the most valuable resource to protect with recruiters."

## The rule

When you draft a reply for Bryan to someone who wants his time (a recruiter, a vendor, a cold
intro), research first, then decide.

- **If the research can already answer the fit question, say the answer.** Do not ask for a call,
  a job description or "more information" to learn something you could find or reasonably guess.
- **Say the verdict up front, with its reason.** For example: the role type fits, but the likely
  customer does not.
- **Give the other person a way to decide without him.** Link the page that says what he wants
  (for job search, https://fryanpan.com/about/#what-im-looking-for) and invite them back only if
  they still see a fit.
- **Offer a call only when the research cannot answer the question and the lead is worth it.**
  Make the call short, and link his booking page instead of proposing times.

## Why

A request for more information hands the research back to Bryan as a meeting. The draft that
prompted this rule asked a recruiter to name the company and send the job description, when the
studio's portfolio already showed the likely customer was an institution, which he does not want.
